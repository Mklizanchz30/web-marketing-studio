# Anti-slop pattern library

Use this as a diagnostic inventory, not a blacklist. Mark a pattern only when the artifact shows it and it causes defaultness, incoherence, repetition, deception, or harm.

## Direction

- The first viewport cannot explain the product or primary action.
- The style could belong to an unrelated product with only the copy changed.
- Several aesthetic systems compete without hierarchy.
- A supplied reference was cloned rather than translated into an original identity.
- New type, color, spacing, radius, shadow, or motion values ignore the local system.
- Decoration communicates a different mood from the content.

## Composition and containers

- Alignment changes accidentally between sections.
- Related items are separated while unrelated items are crowded.
- Every section has identical padding regardless of content.
- Desktop simply collapses into a long mobile stack without reprioritization.
- Content clips, overlaps, overflows, or creates accidental horizontal scrolling.
- Every paragraph, metric, feature, or action is placed in a card.
- Cards are nested without expressing hierarchy.
- Pills, rounded rectangles, borders, rings, and shadows are used by default.
- A bento grid exists because it is fashionable rather than structurally useful.

## Typography and copy

- Tracked uppercase eyebrows appear above most headings.
- Labels, headings, captions, and buttons repeat the same information.
- Long centered paragraphs or forced line breaks resist scanning and reflow.
- Tiny type, excessive tracking, or decorative italics substitute for hierarchy.
- Generic superlatives and buzzwords replace specific actions and outcomes.
- Default hero, logo wall, metrics, features, testimonials, FAQ, and CTA appear regardless of the actual journey.

## Color, material, media, and proof

- Gradients, glow, glass, borders, blur, shadow, noise, and decorative grids stack on the same surface.
- Accent colors have no semantic or brand role.
- Stock or generated imagery is presented as product or customer evidence.
- Generic illustrations, fake dashboards, browser chrome, or icon tiles fill space without meaning.
- Metrics, testimonials, customers, ratings, awards, logos, or activity are invented or unverifiable.
- Image crops lose the subject or fail across breakpoints.

## Interaction and motion

- Several primary actions compete.
- Active, focus, loading, empty, error, disabled, selected, or success states are missing.
- Essential content appears only on hover.
- Every element receives the same reveal.
- Marquees, pulsing statuses, parallax, scale-on-hover, or smooth scrolling add theater without explaining state or narrative.
- Content begins hidden and stays hidden if JavaScript fails.
- Reduced motion only shortens animation instead of showing a stable complete state.

## High-signal clusters

- **Generic AI SaaS:** tracked eyebrow, oversized vague headline, purple gradient, radial lights, glass cards, icon tiles, and bento grid.
- **Card apocalypse:** section shell, nested cards, pill labels, redundant borders, separate shadows, and extreme radii.
- **Motion theater:** hidden-at-rest content, identical reveals, marquee, pulsing status, hover scale, and no reduced-motion path.
- **Fake sophistication:** monospaced micro-labels, decorative grid, fake dashboard, invented metrics, and technical copy without product evidence.
- **Polish before truth:** effects conceal unclear copy, weak actions, missing states, broken responsiveness, or unsupported proof.

Fix the root cause once. Do not report every repeated symptom as a separate idea.
