# Criteria and sources

This reference supports creative risk control, not legal advice. Copyright and trademark rules vary by jurisdiction and facts.

## Evidence-backed boundaries

### Ideas and expression are not the same category

**Criterion:** Separate general ideas, methods, common elements, and familiar design devices from the source's particular expression and arrangement. Do not treat that distinction as permission to copy a recognizable work.

**Source:** [U.S. Copyright Office: Circular 33, Works Not Protected by Copyright](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf).

Circular 33 explains categories that copyright does not protect, including ideas, methods, systems, names, short phrases, and familiar symbols or designs. A particular image, text, or selection and arrangement may still contain protected expression.

### New versions need original authorship

**Criterion:** A revision should add substantial new creative expression rather than make only minor or editorial changes.

**Source:** [U.S. Copyright Office: Circular 14, Copyright in Derivative Works and Compilations](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ14.pdf).

This supports replacing the source identity, composition, subject, and motif relationships. It does not define a universal visual distance threshold.

### Visual works can contain protected expression

**Criterion:** Treat photographs, graphic art, illustrations, and other pictorial works as potentially protected; confirm ownership or permission before reusing actual source assets.

**Source:** [U.S. Copyright Office: Circular 40, Copyright Registration for Pictorial, Graphic, and Sculptural Works](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ40.pdf).

### Names and brand identifiers raise separate issues

**Criterion:** Do not reuse a source brand's marks or create an identity likely to be mistaken for it. Trademark analysis is distinct from copyright analysis.

**Source:** [USPTO: Trademark basics](https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/tm-basics-learn-fundamentals.pdf).

### Current image-generation mechanics

**Criterion:** Use the available image-generation or editing path appropriate to the task, supply reference inputs explicitly, and verify outputs rather than assuming exact text or identity preservation.

**Source:** [OpenAI: Image generation guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-generation).

## Creative heuristics

The relationship modes, visual-grammar/exclusion split, concept matrix, safe-area planning, and preference for typesetting exact text outside generated imagery are workflow heuristics. They do not measure infringement or guarantee clearance.
